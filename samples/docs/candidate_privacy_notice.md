# Candidate Privacy Notice — AcmeHire Ltd

**Last updated: January 2024**

## Who We Are

AcmeHire Ltd (registered in Ireland, Company No. 123456) is the data controller
for personal data collected during our recruitment process.
Contact: privacy@acmehire.com

## What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data from job applicants:

- Full name, email address, phone number, postal address
- CV / résumé, cover letter, work history, education history
- LinkedIn profile URL
- References provided by the applicant

## Why We Collect It

We process your data to:

- Assess your suitability for the position applied for
- Contact you during the recruitment process
- Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

## Who We Share Your Data With

We may share your data with:

- Our internal HR team and hiring managers
- Our recruitment platform provider, who processes data on our behalf

We do not sell your data to third parties.

## How Long We Keep Your Data

Unsuccessful applicants: data is held for 12 months from the date of the decision,
after which it is securely deleted.

If your application is successful, your data is transferred to your employee record
and retained in accordance with our employee data retention policy.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of your data in certain circumstances
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) at www.dataprotection.ie

To exercise your rights, contact privacy@acmehire.com.

## Legal Basis

We process your data on the basis of:

- **Legitimate interests**: assessing candidates for employment
- **Pre-contractual steps**: taking steps at your request prior to entering a contract
- **Legal obligation**: where required by law

## Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version is always available
at www.acmehire.com/privacy.
