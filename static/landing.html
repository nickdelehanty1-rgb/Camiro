The problem

Three gaps that widen without anyone noticing.

Code vs documents

Your privacy notice was approved by legal. Your code was shipped by engineering. Nobody checked whether they describe the same system. Camiro checks.

Agents vs governance

AI agents now take actions autonomously — they send emails, reject candidates, update records. Do your governance documents describe what they can actually do?

Drift vs review

Code changes weekly. Privacy notices are reviewed annually. AI systems are added between audit cycles. The gap between technical reality and compliance documents widens silently.

How it works

Deterministic evidence first. AI second.

1

Scan technical reality

Camiro reads your code, AI agent configurations, and action logs. Ten deterministic scanners and AST-based data flow tracing identify what actually happens — which data flows where, which tools agents can call, and whether oversight controls are present. No AI is involved at this stage.

2

Scan compliance evidence

Upload your privacy notice, DPIA, Data Processing Agreement, or AI policy document. Camiro classifies the document, extracts structured evidence fields, and maps claims to GDPR and EU AI Act obligations. Results are labelled as observed, not evidenced, or requires review.

3

Find the gaps

Camiro compares technical reality against compliance evidence and produces article-cited review areas for your legal, privacy, and AI governance teams. Each finding specifies: what was detected in code, what the document claims, which article is relevant, and that human review is required.

Privacy by design: Raw code is redacted before any AI model is called. Deterministic scanners extract structured findings first. Code content never leaves your session.

Scanners first, AI second

Ten deterministic scanners run before any AI model is invoked. Code is redacted, structured findings are extracted, and only those findings — not raw code — reach the language model for mapping to legal obligations.

Observed vs inferred

AST-verified data flows are labelled as observed technical facts. Pattern-match findings are labelled as inferred signals. The distinction is explicit in every finding. Reviewers know what was proven in code and what was inferred from patterns.

Evidence gaps, not verdicts

Camiro produces article-cited evidence review areas for human legal, privacy, and AI governance teams. It does not determine compliance status. Every finding carries a clear statement: human review required.
